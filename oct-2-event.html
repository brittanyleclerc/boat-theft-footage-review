1. Summary

  • A silver, boxy SUV with a black roof came up the front drive at 11:12 PM and left at 11:23 PM. The plate is not readable in any frame.
  • Five people: three walked onto the dock (A, B, C below) and two waited outside the dock gate (D, E).
  • The three on the dock spent about 2½ minutes there (about 11:15:55 to 11:18:05). They looked at the Grady-White and the lifts, and two of them held phones up as if taking photos or video.
  • Nobody climbed aboard the Grady-White. The boat stayed raised and covered. One man put a hand on the arm of the empty lift seen on DOCK 2; that lift did not move.
  • Hands look empty on the way out. No long, speargun-sized item is seen.
  • DOCK 3 has clear colour views of all three faces at 11:16:14 and 11:17:50 (section 4).

5. Limits, audio and the original video

  • Recording gaps: FRONT DRIVE 11:14–11:22:55 and DOCK PTZ 11:21:13–11:22:37. Nothing can be said about those minutes. No other camera filled them in (section 2). The likely reason is that these cameras record only when their motion detection triggers, and at night, at a distance or outside the camera's view, people and a slow vehicle may not trigger it. This could not be confirmed without opening the recording settings, which this review does not do; the system owner can check the motion sensitivity and schedule for FRONT DRIVE and DOCK PTZ.
  • Audio: DOCK 2 and DOCK 3 show a microphone (talk-back) button in DW Spectrum, so those cameras have audio hardware. Whether sound was recorded could not be checked: this review only looks at the picture, and no settings were opened. Playing the clip with speakers on, or asking the system owner, would settle it.
  • Resolution: frames 1–16 are screen captures; frames 17–23 come from the native 4K DOCK 3 export. A native DW export of 11:12–11:24 PM from DOCK 3, DOCK PTZ and FRONT DRIVE would keep the camera's full resolution and timestamps, and is what the police will want.
